Word2Vec is an algorithm which was developed by Tomas Mikolov and a team of fellow researchers at Google in 2013.
Their paper, Efficient Estimation of Word Representation in Vector Space introduced the Skip-gram architectures.

Later, the negative sampling was introduced in another research paper named Distributed Representations of Words and Phrases and their Compositionality to make the training more efficient.

## Features
- Text preprocessing and vocabulary mapping
- Centre-context pair generation
- Negative sampling using word frequencies raised to 0.75
- Training on CPU or compatible NVIDIA GPUs
- Cosine similarity between learned word embeddings
- Training loss visualization
